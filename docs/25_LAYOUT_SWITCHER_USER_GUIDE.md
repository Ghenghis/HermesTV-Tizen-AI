# Changing How Your TV Looks — Sherri's Guide

---

## You're in control of how HermesTV looks!

You can change the look of HermesTV **anytime you want**. Each style shows the same channels and movies — just in a different way. Don't worry about picking the wrong one. You can always switch back!

---

## How to Open the Layout Switcher

Look at the **top of the screen** for a button that says **"🎨 Look"**.

Just click or press that button, and a menu will pop up showing all the different looks you can choose from.

---

## The 7 Looks You Can Choose

### Mom Mode ⭐ (Recommended for You, Sherri!)

Big, easy-to-read text. Warm pink colors. Simple, clear buttons. This one was made just with you in mind. It's the easiest look for everyday watching and it never feels too busy.

**This is your default — and it's always a good choice to come back to.**

---

### TiviMate

Looks like a classic cable TV guide. Your channels are laid out in a grid, and you can see what's on right now. If you ever miss flipping through channels, this one will feel familiar.

---

### Netflix

Dark background with a big picture at the top showing what's popular. You scroll through rows of movies and shows, just like the Netflix app. Easy to browse and very cozy to look at.

---

### Plex

Gold and grey colors, like a beautiful movie library. Great for when you want to browse through your movie collection and find something to watch tonight.

---

### Apple TV

Very clean and simple, with large pictures. There's not a lot of clutter on the screen — just the content, big and clear. Easy on the eyes.

---

### Samsung Tizen

Looks just like your Samsung Smart TV normally looks! If you're used to the Samsung home screen, this will feel right at home.

---

### Dave Power

Dave's favorite look — it shows a lot of information at once, including extra details on the side of the screen. It can feel a bit busy, but Dave loves it. You're welcome to try it!

---

## If You Ever Get Confused — Hermes Has You

Just say to the Hermes chat bubble:

> **"Mom Mode"**

And it will switch right back to your usual comfortable look. No need to find any buttons.

---

## Ask Hermes to Switch for You

You can also just **talk to the Hermes chat bubble** at any time. Try saying things like:

- *"Change to Netflix look"*
- *"Switch to TiviMate"*
- *"Mom Mode please"*
- *"Show me the Apple TV style"*

DaveTV will understand and switch it for you right away.

---

## If Something Doesn't Look Right

Press the **gear icon (⚙️)** anywhere on the screen and choose **"Reset to defaults"**. That will bring everything back to normal.

You can also call Dave — he set this all up and loves helping you get it just right!

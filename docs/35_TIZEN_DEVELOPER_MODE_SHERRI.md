# 35 — Putting HermesTV on Your TV

**For Mom (Sherri).**

Hi Mom — this is just so you know what's going on while I put HermesTV on
your TV. **You don't have to do anything.** I'll be at your TV with the
remote for a few minutes. You can keep doing whatever you're doing.

This page is short on purpose.

---

## What you might see

1. A small note in the corner of the TV that says **"Developer Mode."**
   That's normal. It means I'm allowed to put new apps on your TV. It
   goes away on its own after a minute.

2. **Your TV might restart once.** Don't worry. It comes right back to
   the channel you were on.

3. **A new icon called "HermesTV."** It looks like a little orange
   square with an **H** on it. When you press OK on it with the remote,
   it opens.

That's it.

---

## What you don't need to worry about

- **Nothing breaks** in your normal TV stuff. Live TV, Netflix, all of
  that is exactly the same.

- **Nothing is being recorded.** This is just an app I made for you.

- **You can't break it by pressing buttons.** Press anything. If
  something looks wrong, just go back to the home screen with the **Home**
  button on the remote, and tell me.

---

## When it's done

I'll tell you "HermesTV is on your TV now." You can try it whenever you
feel like it. Press **Home**, find the orange **H** icon, and press OK.

The TV will say hi using my voice (well, a voice I picked — see, no
Bixby talking back to you, I promise).

If anything ever looks weird, **just hit the Home button on the remote**.
The TV always comes back to normal.

Love you.
— Dave
